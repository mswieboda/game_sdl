# Quadruped Templates and Animations

Available templates for `body_type='quadruped'`:
- `bear`
- `cat`
- `dog`
- `horse`
- `lion`

Animations vary by template. Use `get_character()` on a created character to see its available animations. Typical animations include:
- `walk`
- `run`
- `idle`
- `attack`
- `eat`
- `sleep`
